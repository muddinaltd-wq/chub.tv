FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What is Chubi?

Chubi is one subscription for live TV, movies, and series — 50,000+ channels and a large on-demand library, on the devices you already own.

Which devices does Chubi work on?

Smart TVs, Fire TV, Android, iPhone and iPad, Apple TV, and any modern web browser. Setup takes a few minutes and our team can walk you through it.

How fast is activation?

Most orders are activated within minutes of checkout. If anything needs a manual check, our team will message you right away.

Can I watch on more than one screen at once?

Yes — pick 1, 2, or 3 devices at checkout. Each plan lists how many simultaneous streams you get.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are prepaid for the term you choose, and you can stop whenever you like.

What does the money-back guarantee cover?

If Chubi isn't right for you, contact support within 7 days of purchase and we'll sort out a refund.

How do I get support?

24/7 chat and WhatsApp. The contact buttons on every plan go straight to our team.
